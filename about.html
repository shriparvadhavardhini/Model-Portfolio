Biography

Five years of editorial, fashion and beauty work

Pooja began modelling in 2021, working first with independent Coimbatore designers before moving into editorial and beauty campaigns. Her range spans high-contrast black and white editorial work, colour-driven fashion stories, natural beauty photography and location-based street shoots.

She approaches every booking as a collaboration — arriving prepared, adaptable on set, and comfortable holding a concept across long shoot days. Recent work includes independent print editorial, seasonal lookbooks and beauty campaign photography.
